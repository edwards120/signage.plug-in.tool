# SignSpec Studio — Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Rules foundation

Status: **In progress**

- Shared TypeScript calculation package
- Sign-face and perimeter calculations
- Vinyl area and waste calculations
- Configurable viewing-distance guidance
- Configurable mounting-height checks
- Estimate, markup, and gross-margin calculations
- Type checking and automated tests

## Phase 1 — Project intake and survey records

- Project, client, and site records
- Survey photographs and notes
- Measurement checklist
- Substrate, access, power, and installation fields
- Missing-information warnings
- Versioned project assumptions

## Phase 2 — Six core sign calculators

- ADA room sign
- Dimensional letters
- Wall cabinet
- Vinyl graphic
- Wayfinding panel
- Monument panel

Each calculator should return:

- Inputs and assumptions
- Calculated quantities
- Preliminary material and mounting notes
- Warning and review flags
- Estimate-ready line items

## Phase 3 — Estimating and proposals

- Material and labor library
- Vendor and shop-specific pricing profiles
- Waste and contingency controls
- Markup versus margin explanation
- Proposal and estimate templates
- Assumptions, exclusions, and alternates
- Branded PDF output

## Phase 4 — Review and approval

- Shareable project summary
- Client comments
- Approval and revision history
- Downloadable sign schedule
- Controlled release status

## Phase 5 — Host-software adapters

Potential adapters, developed only after the shared rules and project model are stable:

- Illustrator
- CorelDRAW
- SketchUp
- CAD or BIM coordination
- Production-management and estimating systems

## Phase 6 — Advanced field intelligence

- Calibrated photo measurement
- Computer-vision assisted survey review
- Drawing and photo comparison
- Duplicate or conflicting measurement detection
- Site-specific knowledge retrieval

AI-assisted features should suggest and flag. They should not silently issue fabrication or compliance decisions.
